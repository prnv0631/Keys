Turn on the microphone, press Start, then play each note as it reaches the line.
How it works
  • Notes fall toward the amber line. The song waits there until you play the right note on your piano.
  • Play one note at a time and let it ring. Repeated notes need a fresh keypress.
  • Want a different song? Use Import MIDI to load any .mid file as falling notes.
  • No microphone? Use the computer keys A S D F G H J K for C to C, and W E T Y U for sharps.

Tip: put your device within a metre of the piano and keep the room quiet. Sound made by this page is ignored by the listener automatically.
